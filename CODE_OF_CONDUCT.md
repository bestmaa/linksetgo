# Community code of conduct

LinksetGo contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation welcoming,
safe, and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Protect private information and coordinate security disclosures responsibly.
- Accept correction, repair harm, and move discussions back to the work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing,
deliberate disruption, and sustained bad-faith conduct are not acceptable in
project spaces or when representing the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other material that
violates these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, prior
behavior, and the safety of affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner or through the private
channel named by the project. Do not post sensitive evidence publicly. Before a
public launch, maintainers must publish a monitored conduct contact and a conflict
of-interest escalation path.

This policy applies in repositories, issue trackers, reviews, community events, and
other spaces officially associated with LinksetGo.
