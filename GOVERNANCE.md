# Relay governance

Relay is currently in its pre-1.0, founder-led stage.

## Decision model

The project owner is accountable for release scope, maintainership, licensing,
trademark use, security response, and compatibility of the permanent public URL
contract. Maintainers may merge ordinary fixes and documentation improvements
after review.

Changes to authentication, tenant isolation, public URL structure, association
files, billing, data retention, license, or trademark policy require a written
design issue and explicit owner approval.

## Project principles

- Keep Community Edition operationally complete and free of artificial quotas.
- Keep Cloud-only vendors behind replaceable provider boundaries.
- Preserve published links and documented mobile contracts across upgrades.
- Default private records to authenticated access and expose narrow public
  projections.
- Publish security, migration, and compatibility effects with every release.
- Do not claim uptime, compliance, customers, or support commitments without
  evidence.

## Maintainers

Maintainer and code-owner identities will be published before the first public
tag. Until then, repository ownership determines final approval. A future move to
shared governance must document nomination, removal, conflict-of-interest, and
appeal procedures before it takes effect.
